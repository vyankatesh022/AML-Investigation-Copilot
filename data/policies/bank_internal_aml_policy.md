# Global Horizon Bank: Internal AML & Transaction Monitoring Policy (Manual Section 4)

**Document Code:** GHB-POL-AML-SEC04-V2  
**Owner:** Financial Crime Compliance (FCC) Division  
**Scope:** Retail Banking, Commercial Banking, Wealth Management, and Digital Payment Channels  

---

## 1. Automated Transaction Monitoring Alerts and Triage Thresholds
All transactions processed through Global Horizon Bank systems are continuously screened against automated detection scenarios:

* **Scenario TM-01: Statutory High-Value Alert:**
  * Trigger: Any single transaction equal to or exceeding $10,000.00 USD (or local currency equivalent).
  * Action: Initial triage within 24 business hours; automated CTR logging.
* **Scenario TM-02: Structuring & Currency Avoidance:**
  * Trigger: Cumulative or single transactions between $9,000.00 and $9,999.99 conducted within 3 consecutive business days.
  * Action: Mandatory analyst review. If intentional structuring is suspected, a Suspicious Activity Report (SAR) recommendation must be formulated.
* **Scenario TM-03: Rapid Velocity & Frequency Bursts:**
  * Trigger: Three (3) or more outward transfers or withdrawals executed from the same account within a 24-hour window.
  * Action: Compare current velocity against customer's trailing 90-day baseline.

---

## 2. Customer Due Diligence (CDD) and Turnover Deviations
Every customer account maintains an approved Expected Monthly Turnover limit established during KYC onboarding.

* **Turnover Deviation Threshold:** Any single transaction or aggregate weekly volume that exceeds 150% of the customer's declared monthly turnover must trigger an automated Alert Review.
* **Inconsistent Activity:** If a retail customer declared as a student or retail worker executes transactions in tens of thousands of dollars, the compliance analyst must issue a Request for Information (RFI) to obtain proof of source of funds (e.g., pay stubs, inheritance documents, sale of property).

---

## 3. Escalation and Disposition Workflow
Upon completing an initial transaction investigation, the analyst must designate one of three standardized dispositions:
1. **Close as False Positive (Disclose Justification):** Validated legitimate commercial explanation with supporting documentation.
2. **Request for Information (RFI):** Temporarily hold or monitor while branch relationship manager requests source of wealth documentation from the client.
3. **Escalate to SAR Committee:** Unexplained structuring, pass-through mule activity, high-risk country wire transfers, or uncooperative customers must be escalated for formal SAR filing.
